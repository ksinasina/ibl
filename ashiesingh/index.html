Professional Summary

Reconnecting performance with humanity.

Ashie Singh is a nationally and internationally recognised organisational wellbeing strategist with over 25 years of experience across Human Resources leadership, applied behavioural science, employee wellbeing, and culture transformation. She specialises in addressing burnout, chronic stress, and work life integration through evidence based, human centred interventions that translate wellbeing into sustainable performance.

As Founder and CEO of InterBeing Leadership (IBL) and sole innovator behind InterBeing Care Connect (ICC), she has developed an integrated approach to workplace wellbeing that combines leadership development, behavioural science, recognition, performance, and culture transformation into one measurable ecosystem. IBL and ICC work in tandem: IBL provides the strategy, facilitation, and leadership development, while ICC provides the platform that makes the work continuous, embedded, and measurable inside organisations.

Her professional journey includes more than a decade with Cummins, where she led talent management, organisational development, and culture transformation across 13 countries in Africa and the Middle East. Earlier, she established the first cross brand training and development function at New Clicks Group.

Alongside her corporate work, Ashie has been senior faculty with the Art of Living Foundation since 1998, teaching Stress Release programmes that combine breathwork, meditation, and yoga. She serves as a Trustee for Art of Living South Africa and is Programme Coordinator for I Meditate Africa, the largest peace meditation event on the continent, held annually to commemorate Africa Day.

She has facilitated wellbeing and leadership programmes across Africa, the Middle East, and Europe, working with executives, managers, and employees in complex, high pressure environments. Ashie is known for her ability to translate complex wellbeing science into practical, engaging, and actionable workshops that produce immediate insight and long term behavioural change.

Methodology

The E-RAMM framework.

InterBeing Leadership's proprietary model for sustainable human performance. Three progressive stages, five core dimensions. The bridge between individual development and organisational transformation.

Stage 1
Leading Self

Developing the self awareness, resilience, and inner clarity that make great leadership possible. The journey begins here.

Stage 2
Leading Teams

Applying inner mastery to build high trust, high performance teams where connection, engagement, and shared purpose thrive.

Stage 3
Leading Organisations

Embedding leadership capability across the whole organisation to create lasting cultural change and Human Energy at every level.

The five dimensions of E-RAMM

E
Engagement

Building genuine, energised commitment, not just compliance, at every level of the organisation.

R
Resilience

Developing the individual and collective capacity to navigate pressure, change, and complexity with agility.

A
Awareness

Cultivating the self knowledge and situational awareness that precede every lasting transformation.

M
Meaning

Connecting daily work to deeper purpose, the engine of sustainable motivation and cultural belonging.

M
Mastery

Tracking growth and building the capabilities and competencies that endure beyond the programme.

E-RAMM sits at the heart of every InterBeing Leadership engagement, making the framework the bridge between individual development and organisational transformation.

Signature Keynotes

Two talks. One belief.

Sustainable organisational performance is not possible when people are exhausted, disconnected, or emotionally depleted. Both keynotes start there, and take leaders in different directions.

Keynote One

Leading Wellbeing in the Workplace

The Shift from Human Resources to Human Energy Management

In today's high pressure workplace, organisations are no longer struggling because people lack skill. They are struggling because people are emotionally depleted, disconnected, and running on survival mode. This keynote challenges leaders to rethink wellbeing not as an HR initiative, but as a leadership responsibility and a strategic business capability.

Ashie explores the shift from traditional Human Resources Management to Human Energy Management, where leaders learn to recognise, protect, and elevate the emotional, mental, and relational energy of their teams.

Her sessions combine behavioural science, neuroscience informed insights, reflective dialogue, practical leadership tools, and deeply human storytelling, creating experiences that inspire leaders to move beyond managing people and into elevating human energy.

Leaders walk away able to
  • Understand how leadership energy directly shapes culture, trust, engagement, and performance
  • Recognise the hidden signs of emotional depletion, disengagement, and burnout within teams
  • Shift from pressure based leadership to energising, human centred leadership
  • Create psychologically safe environments where people feel seen, valued, and connected
  • Build cultures of care, collaboration, clarity, and accountability without compromising performance
  • Lead with greater self awareness, emotional regulation, and intentional presence
  • Understand how wellbeing influences how employees show up in their families, communities, and society
Keynote Two

Addressing Burnout, Stress, and Work Life Balance

For Sustainable Employee Wellness

A high impact, interactive keynote and workshop format that gives leaders and employees the language, the tools, and the permission to talk about burnout differently. Ashie shifts the conversation from individual fragility to shared organisational responsibility, then back again to personal practice.

The session combines psychology, neuroscience informed practices, reflective dialogue, and practical micro habits. Participants leave with immediate insight and with practices they can use the next morning, not three months later.

Leaders walk away able to
  • Recognise early and advanced signs of burnout and chronic stress
  • Use practical tools for emotional regulation, energy management, and boundary setting
  • Shift mindsets from survival mode productivity to sustainable performance
  • Build personal accountability for wellbeing without organisational blame
  • Create shared language and collective ownership around wellbeing
